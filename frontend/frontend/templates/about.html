About the Project

Detection-Guided Deep Unfolding for Joint Underwater Image Enhancement & Object Detection

Underwater Vision Challenges

Underwater images suffer from severe quality degradation due to light absorption, scattering, and color distortion. These effects not only reduce visual quality but also drastically impact the performance of downstream object detection models. Traditional enhancement methods often treat enhancement and detection as separate tasks, leading to suboptimal results for high-level vision.

Our framework unifies both objectives through a Detection-Guided Deep Unfolding mechanism, where enhancement is explicitly steered to maximize detection confidence. This end-to-end learnable architecture adapts to real-world turbid water conditions.

The Core Insight

Instead of performing enhancement and detection in isolation, we use a deep unfolding strategy that iteratively refines the latent clear image guided by detection loss gradients. This creates a virtuous cycle: better enhancement enables more accurate detection, which in turn informs the enhancement module.

Methodology: Deep Unfolding + YOLO11m

Deep Unfolding Module

We formulate underwater image formation as an optimization problem and unroll iterative proximal gradient steps into a learnable neural network. Each stage estimates the clear image and residual degradation, supervised by detection feedback.

YOLO11m Detector

State-of-the-art real-time object detector with enhanced feature extraction. The detection head back-propagates task-specific gradients to the unfolding network, aligning enhancement with detection objectives.

Cross‑Scale Attention

Our attention mechanism captures multi-scale contextual information, preserving fine details while suppressing artifacts. This is critical for detecting small marine objects in degraded scenes.

Training Data

Trained on the UIEB (Underwater Image Enhancement Benchmark) and SQUID dataset, containing diverse real-world underwater scenes with paired reference images and annotated objects (fish, divers, trash, etc.).

Performance Highlights

  • mAP@0.5: 0.7806 (outperforms baseline by +8.2%)
  • Precision: 0.8124  |  Recall: 0.748
  • Inference speed: 22 ms per image (RTX 4090)
  • Joint enhancement improves detection of small objects by +14.3% compared to separate pipeline.

Why Detection-Guided Deep Unfolding?

Conventional underwater enhancement methods (histogram equalization, GAN-based, or physical models) are not optimized for detection. They might introduce over‑smoothing or color casts that confuse detectors. By explicitly incorporating detection loss into the unfolding iterations, our network learns to enhance only the visual characteristics that boost detection confidence — a form of task‑driven adaptation.

This paradigm is especially valuable for autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs), marine biology monitoring, and underwater robotics, where real‑time and accurate detection is crucial despite poor visibility.
